DATA SOURCE

Train with your CSV

Upload labelled payments to train and evaluate a model. Export your review log first: training replaces the model and clears active events.

Website uploads use the simpler CSV training workflow. They do not reproduce the full-stream sequence model.

Dataset requirements

Required: step, type, amount, nameOrig, nameDest, isFraud. Optional: id. Sender and recipient are accepted aliases. At least 100 rows, 10 time steps, and both classes in each time split.

Balances, labels and fraud flags never enter prediction features. Same-hour file order does not establish actual sub-hour timing.
